# Chronos D0 M6.3–M6.7 design

Scope: one run, portfolio, account, listing and quote currency; BacktestPaperReplay only. Preserve separate target, risk, reservation, execution, broker, ledger, valuation authorities. No live routing, network broker, full lifecycle/recovery framework, reconciliation service, multi-leg, funding, FX, or database. Existing M6.2 immutable RiskDecision already records risk_sequence, projected_exposure_id, authorized delta, source target and policy versions. Keep it authoritative; do not repeat its 1,000-line admission engine downstream.

## Dependency and shared contracts

New small contracts headers hold paper facts and settled exposure evidence: contracts/include/chronos/contracts/paper_execution.hpp and accounting.hpp. Use existing IDs or add typed IDs to value_objects.hpp as needed. Shared structs use existing fixed-point, RunMode, StateLineage, VersionRef and identity types. Facts have full run/account/portfolio/instrument/listing scope, sequence/logical time, source identities and policy versions. Authority outputs should be immutable via read-only access or const views; input DTOs may be aggregates. Each authority validates input and commits its complete next state only after all checks/arithmetic succeed.

core/risk owns reservations. core/execution owns intent eligibility. adapters/paper owns broker simulation, consuming shared contracts rather than depending on accounting. accounting/ owns ledger and valuation; confirm repository owning directory README before implementation. Applications compose dependencies; no core-to-adapter or core-to-accounting implementation edge. Neutral SettledExposureEvidence carries full scope, ledger cursor, ledger checksum, position, scale and quality; reservation consumes this contract through explicit reconciliation, never an accounting class.

## Capacity serialization

ReservationAuthority is noncopyable, single-writer and bound to one complete scope, scale, run mode, cap and matching risk/limit/exposure model versions. snapshot(cut) produces existing ProjectedExposureSnapshot. Initial position is declared and zero in D0; later position must come from ledger evidence. Reservation request takes immutable RiskDecision and stable request identity. Validate decision authorizes target, complete scope, mode, policy, expiry and delta consistency. Require exact snapshot identity, risk_sequence and projected-before equality. Stale returns to risk; no mutation of decision. Cap is configured independently and version-bound because RiskDecision does not expose the policy cap directly.

Accept computes checked projected + authorized_delta and enforces absolute cap; hold includes authorized signed delta, source target/decision/outcome, expiry and mode. Opposing holds must not hide worst-case exposure: simplest D0 policy permits at most one unsettled reservation per listing, rejecting another until terminal reconciliation. Identical retry returns identical stored outcome; same identity with changed semantic payload conflicts. Different request identity cannot reserve same decision twice. Sequence increases on accepted state transitions and never wraps.

Consume binds one intent identity and does not free capacity. Release/expiry applies only before consumption. A consumed hold stays pending until a scoped ledger cursor proves its fill has posted; reconcile atomically replaces held delta with ledger-derived settled position. Posting failure leaves capacity held and stops downstream processing. D0 full-fill sequential pipeline avoids partial/unknown order machinery. Reject out-of-order settlement, listing mismatch and duplicate conflicting evidence.

## Execution and broker

ExecutionAuthority creates ExecutableOrderIntent only from approved/modified risk and the matching currently active reservation through reservation authority consumption. Prevalidate all fields before consume. Narrow synchronous create/consume handoff must be transactional: failed consume creates no eligible intent. Intent includes exact authorized signed delta converted to side and positive quantity (checked INT64_MIN), full scope, risk/target/reservation IDs, StateLineage, price basis, expiry and model policy. Only BacktestPaperReplay accepted, even though broader planning permits LivePaper later.

PaperBroker accepts typed intents and scoped fresh market cut. Deterministic immediate full fill at ask for buy, bid for sell; configurable nonnegative tick slippage, integer fee rate and logical ack/fill latency offsets. Fill price must remain positive/on tick, quantity on step, and checked latency remains within declared eligibility. A delayed timestamp is a model offset at the supplied cut, not a claim to use future market data. Record this explicitly. No sleeping, wall time or randomness needed. Return ack and fill with immutable lineage and model versions; invalid market or unsupported mode produces explicit rejection and no fill. Same intent retries do not create another order/fill; conflicts reject. Fee in quote Money travels in fill and is posted once by ledger.

## Ledger and valuation

Versioned D0 spot-like quantity/quote accounting policy: ledger balances each unit separately. Fill transaction records position quantity versus quantity clearing, quote cash versus trade clearing, and fee expense versus quote cash. Signed entry amounts sum zero per unit/currency; quantity and quote must never be added to prove balance. Include signed fill quantity, exact trade notional, fee, source fill and lineage in transaction semantics so projections rebuild solely from transactions. Checked fixed-point multiply/rescale/round policy applies to notional and fee; never float. Explicit opening cash transaction optional; zero opening position required initially.

Ledger post is atomic and idempotent by economic fill identity plus complete payload. Conflicting duplicate fails. Append-only reverse(original_tx_id,correction_id) appends exact negated entries with source linkage once, never edits original. Unknown/already compensated/conflicting compensation rejects. Projection must account for compensation; rebuild effective posting stream with explicit reversed originals excluded, rather than naively treating a reversal as a new trade at a misleading cost basis. No partial mutation on overflow.

Position and P&L derive from ledger only. FIFO lots is a simple exact policy: maintain signed lots with price/notional basis, close opposing quantities, realize close notional minus allocated basis (sign-aware), leave residual lot on reversal. Use deterministic residual allocation when partial quantity divides lot cost nonexactly; no dropped money. Fees are separate cumulative posted expense; net total = realized gross + unrealized gross - fees. Hit rate counts completed closing events with positive net allocated P&L under explicitly documented allocation (or report gross hit rate clearly). Marks identify listing, currency, source view/StateLineage, logical time, quality, mark/rounding policy. Missing/stale/future/mismatched marks yield unavailable valuation, never numeric zero masquerading as value.

## Composition

M6.7 adds executable replay runner over existing captured-dataset and replay-provider APIs. For each published view feed existing feature/strategy/recommendation, construct target from last posted ledger position, risk using reservation snapshot, reserve, create/consume intent, broker, ledger, reconcile, derive mark/summary. Same input cut remains the causation cut; local authority sequences remain separate. Failure stops affected work, retains held capacity where unresolved, and emits explicit status. Summary includes full chain IDs, ledger balance/cursor/checksum, position, gross/net P&L, fees, marks and versions. Run twice and compare semantic output. Synthetic fixture is a deterministic test, not represented as captured real data.

Verified accounting/README.md: accounting accepts contracts and narrow persistence ports only; must not depend on market-state, strategy, or UI. Use existing accounting/ledger and accounting/valuation submodules, and shared mark DTO. Existing replay API is runtime/datasets/include/chronos/runtime/datasets/replay.hpp.
